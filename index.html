How to Play 🍁

Objective

Exercise your visual recall by matching all 8 curling and winter championship pairs on the 4×4 sheet grid in as few turns as possible.

Gameplay Rules

  • One Tap Flip: Tap any card to reveal its custom symbol and label.
  • Find the Match: Reveal a second card. If they match, they lock face-up with a verified checkmark badge.
  • Mismatches: If the cards do not match, memorize their positions before they turn back face down.
  • Daily Release: A brand new daily challenge unlocks every night at midnight UK time (Europe/London).

Star Ratings

  • ★★★ Masterful Recall: 12 turns or fewer
  • ★★☆ Great Match: 13–18 turns
  • ★☆☆ Solved: 19+ turns
